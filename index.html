Welcome to Bible High

Where Old Testament and New Testament tales collide.

Welcome to Bible High, home of the Lions! Moses, Esther, David, Jonah, Jesus, Mary, Joseph, and many more students all walk the same hallways, and their Old and New Testament stories collide in funny, modern school-day adventures about belonging, teamwork, and grace. It’s made for young readers and the families who read together to connect.

Every story is based on a real Bible account and is packed with Easter eggs on purpose. At the end of each one, we show readers exactly where to find the original in Scripture, so families can open the Good Book and read it together. The setting is new, but the lessons remain. Each issue is $2.99 on Kindle.

You can also watch Bible High on our YouTube series. The Bible High universe is growing in every way, with new stories, new characters, and new places to meet them. We’d love your help shaping it. Share your ideas for growing the Bible High universe in the Share Your Ideas box below!
